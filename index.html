FAQ

How do I order?

Pick a design and your size, add it to your bag, then choose your delivery option at checkout and pay by card.

What is BOX NOW?

A network of parcel lockers across Cyprus and Greece, open 24/7. Your shirt is delivered to the locker you choose, and you collect it whenever it suits you with the code you receive. No waiting at home for a courier.

Can I collect my order in person?

Yes, if you're in Limassol, local pickup is free. We'll get in touch to arrange a time and place once your shirt is ready.

How long will it take?

Your shirt is printed to order in Limassol, then sent with the delivery option you chose. We'll let you know as soon as it's on its way, with tracking where the service offers it.

How do I pay?

By card at checkout. We don't offer cash on delivery.

How is it printed?

With DTF (direct-to-film) printing, which gives sharp, bright colours that flex with the fabric. Wash cold and inside out to keep the print looking new.

What if something is wrong with my order?

Get in touch with us. If your shirt arrives damaged, misprinted or in the wrong size, we'll sort it out. Please double-check your address or locker at checkout, since parcels sent to a wrong address come back to us.
